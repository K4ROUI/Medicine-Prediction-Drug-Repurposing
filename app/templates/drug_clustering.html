About Clustering Analysis

This tool groups drugs or medicines based on their properties:

  • Drug Properties - Clusters drugs based on molecular weight, hydrogen donors/acceptors, rotatable bonds, and LogP value.
  • Medicine Uses - Groups medicines with similar therapeutic indications using text analysis.
  • Medicine Side Effects - Identifies patterns in reported side effects across different medicines.

The visualizations use dimensionality reduction (PCA) to plot high-dimensional data in 2D space while preserving patterns.
