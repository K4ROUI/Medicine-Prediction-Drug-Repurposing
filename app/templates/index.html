About the App

This application combines multiple AI models for medicine analysis:

  • Medicine Use Predictor: Identifies potential therapeutic applications of medicines based on their name and composition.
  • Side Effect Predictor: Predicts potential side effects that might occur with a given medicine.
  • Drug Repurposing Predictor: Discovers new therapeutic uses for existing drugs, potentially shortening drug development timelines.

All predictions are based on machine learning models trained on comprehensive medicine datasets. The accuracy of predictions depends on the quality of the training data and the specific model parameters.
