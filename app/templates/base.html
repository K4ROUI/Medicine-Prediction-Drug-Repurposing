About Model Evaluation

Model evaluation allows you to assess the performance of our prediction models using test datasets.

When you evaluate a model, the following metrics are calculated:

  • Accuracy: Overall correctness of predictions
  • Precision: Ratio of correct positive predictions
  • Recall: Ability to find all positive instances
  • F1 Score: Harmonic mean of precision and recall
  • ROC AUC: Area under the ROC curve (for binary classification)

Results are presented as both numerical metrics and visual charts for easier interpretation.
